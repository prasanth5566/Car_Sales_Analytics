# Car_Sales_Analytics
Power BI + SQL dashboard for cars sales analytics.

**🚗 Cars Sales Analytics Dashboard (Power BI + SQL)**
Interactive analytics for revenue, orders, and product performance across car types, segments, and models—built with Power BI and backed by SQL.

**📌 Project Description / Purpose**
This Power BI solution analyzes cars sales performance to answer:
How are revenue, orders, and quantity trending daily and monthly?
Which car types/segments drive the most sales?
What are the top/bottom performers by revenue, quantity, and orders?
All core calculations (KPIs and charts) are documented via SQL so results are auditable and reproducible.

**🛠 Tech Stack**
Power BI Desktop – Data modeling & interactive visuals
SQL (MySQL-compatible) – KPI & trend queries used for validation/replication 
Data – CSV + Excel (converted from your CSV)
TomTom Map (Power BI) – Geographic visuals (optional)

**📂 Data Source**
Dataset Name: Car Sales Data
Files Included:
car_sales_data.csv (raw data)
car_sales_data.xlsx (Excel version)
Key Fields: Order Date, Car Type, Car Segment, Car Model, Quantity, Total Price, Order ID

**✨ Features / Highlights**
1️⃣ Business Problem:
The automobile company needed a centralized reporting solution to monitor:
Daily & monthly sales performance
Top & bottom-performing car models
Sales contribution by type (SUV, Sedan, Truck, Coupe, etc.) and segment (Compact, Midsize, Heavy Duty, Sport)

2️⃣ Goal of Dashboard:
Track key KPIs like Total Revenue, Avg Order Value, Cars Sold, Orders, Avg Cars per Order
Visualize daily and monthly order trends
Compare car types and segments by sales share
Identify top 5 and bottom 5 cars by revenue, orders, and quantity

3️⃣ Walkthrough of Key Visuals:
KPI Cards (Top): Total Revenue, Avg Order Value, Cars Sold, Orders, Avg Cars per Order
Daily Trend (Bar Chart): Orders by day of the week
Monthly Trend (Line Chart): Orders trend across months
Donut Charts: % of sales by Car Type and Car Segment
Bar Chart: Cars sold by Car Type
Top/Bottom Sellers Page:
Top 5 models by Revenue, Quantity, and Orders
Bottom 5 models by Revenue, Quantity, and Orders

4️⃣ Business Impact & Insights:
Revenue Drivers: Coupe and Truck categories contribute highest revenue.
Trend Monitoring: Orders peak midweek (Wednesdays, Mondays).
Segment Mix: Sport and Compact segments lead in sales contribution.
Inventory Actions: Bottom-performing models identified for potential discounting or removal.
